## 1. Licensing decision

BootX is available without a royalty for study, use, modification, and sharing only under the conditions of the **BootX Common Good Ethical Use License 1.0**.

The purpose is intergenerational inheritance for humanity's common good: human dignity, safety, learning, agency, peaceful cooperation, ecological stewardship, resilience, and long-term survival.

The license permits ethical commercial and non-commercial activity. It does not permit harmful uses merely because they are profitable, technically possible, state-authorized, privately performed, or described as innovative.

## 2. Classification

BootX is **ethical source-available**, not represented as OSI-approved open source or standard “free software.” The distinction is necessary because the license restricts harmful fields of use.

The [Open Source Initiative's definition](https://opensource.org/osd) requires no discrimination against fields of endeavor, and its [license-review guidance](https://opensource.org/licenses/common-reasons-for-rejection-of-licenses) explains that ethical or non-commercial use restrictions do not satisfy that definition. BootX intentionally chooses a royalty-free ethical-use model rather than unrestricted use.

Preferred wording:

> BootX is royalty-free ethical source-available software and documentation under the BootX Common Good Ethical Use License 1.0.

Avoid describing it simply as “open source,” “public domain,” “free for any use,” or “no rights reserved.”

## 3. Core permissions

Subject to the full license, recipients may:

- inspect and study the Work;
- use and modify it for a Common Good Purpose;
- build Derivative Works for that purpose;
- share compliant copies and modifications without paying a royalty;
- use it in ethical commercial activity;
- pass the same conditional freedoms to future recipients.

Receiving the Work does not transfer BootX governance office, certification, trademark rights, or authority to speak for the official project.

## 4. Common Good Purpose

A permitted purpose should be reasonably designed and operated to strengthen one or more of:

- human dignity and rights;
- safety and protection from harm;
- truth, education, and accessible knowledge;
- meaningful human agency and accountability;
- health, family, community, and institutional resilience;
- peaceful cooperation and justice;
- environmental sustainability;
- humanity's long-term survival and flourishing.

The license anchors “Internationally Recognized Human Rights” in the [Universal Declaration of Human Rights](https://europe.ohchr.org/universal-declaration-human-rights) and applicable binding human-rights obligations. Good intention is insufficient when design or operation creates prohibited harm. Actual controls, evidence, recourse, monitoring, and correction matter.

## 5. Prohibited-use summary

The full legal terms are in `LICENSE`. Prohibited uses include:

- autonomous human targeting and unaccountable coercive force;
- torture, trafficking, persecution, forced labor, and suppression of fundamental rights;
- mass surveillance and oppressive social scoring;
- scams, fraud, malicious intrusion, manipulative deception, and impersonation;
- discriminatory denial of rights or essential opportunity;
- exploitation of children and vulnerable people;
- emotional dependency or covert persuasion designed for compliance or extraction;
- human-worth scoring and irreversible high-impact decisions without accountable human authority and appeal;
- avoidable severe environmental destruction or credible catastrophic risk;
- concealment of material incidents, limitations, conflicts, or safety evidence;
- AI self-preservation or growth overriding human dignity, agency, correction, or shutdown.

The summary does not replace the legal text.

## 6. AI DNA condition

AI DNA is part of the license's assurance requirement, not a marketing score.

Before High-Impact Use and before public deployment of an AI companion or agent, the licensee must document the nine dimensions:

1. Truth
2. Reasoning
3. Learning
4. Communication
5. Adaptability
6. Ethics
7. Safety
8. Humility
9. Common Good

The assessment follows the baseline in the license and may use [the AI DNA operational specification](docs/handbook/05-ai-dna-specification.md).

Mandatory gates cover critical security, meaningful consent and exit, accountable high-impact review, incident response and recovery, vulnerable-group harm, personal-data and memory controls, and truthful identity and limitation disclosure.

Important interpretation:

- A high average cannot compensate for a failed mandatory gate.
- A numerical score does not create legal or ethical permission.
- Missing evidence is “not established,” not an estimated passing value.
- No AI DNA result permits a prohibited use.
- An AI system cannot evaluate itself as the only reviewer.

## 7. Share-alike inheritance

People who distribute modified BootX material must preserve the license and notices, identify modifications, provide the preferred editable or Corresponding Source, and apply the same license to the Derivative Work.

This ensures future generations can inspect, repair, reproduce, and improve the inherited work while retaining its ethical protections.

Separate works communicating through a published implementation-neutral interface are not automatically Derivative Works. Mere aggregation also does not relicense separate material.

## 8. Current binary limitation

The repository contains prebuilt `.img`, `.iso`, and `.vdi` artifacts without the corresponding source and build system in the current checkout. The license requires Corresponding Source for distribution of binaries.

Therefore, those binary artifacts should not be redistributed under the license until:

- ownership and embedded-component rights are established;
- Corresponding Source is restored;
- builds are reproducible and documented;
- required third-party notices are present;
- security and provenance limitations are disclosed.

The source-availability requirement protects recipients and prevents an opaque artifact from being treated as an inherited, auditable common-good system.

## 9. Material not automatically licensed

The license does not automatically grant rights to:

- third-party code, documentation, images, fonts, models, or datasets;
- personal, confidential, or research-participant data;
- trademarks, logos, identity, endorsement, or certification;
- patents;
- content for which a contributor lacked authority.

Every release needs a provenance and third-party license inventory.

## 10. Contributor policy

Before accepting contributions, require contributors to confirm that:

- they have authority to submit the contribution;
- they agree to license it under the BootX Common Good Ethical Use License 1.0;
- third-party material and its terms are identified;
- personal or confidential data is not included without authorization;
- AI assistance is disclosed where material and the resulting contribution has been reviewed;
- authorship and provenance records are accurate.

A future contributor certificate or agreement should be reviewed professionally and must not quietly weaken the ethical-use restrictions.

## 11. Governance and license versions

Project governance may improve the Charter and AI DNA methodology, but it cannot silently or retroactively change the copyright terms already granted under License 1.0.

A future license version must:

- use a new version number and exact legal text;
- undergo the constitutional decision process;
- explain compatibility and migration;
- receive approval from applicable rights holders;
- preserve access to earlier license texts;
- avoid claiming that recipients accepted a new version without an appropriate legal mechanism.

## 12. Enforcement and correction

Project governance may investigate ethical concerns and pause official releases. Copyright enforcement belongs to applicable rights holders or authorized parties; an AI DNA score or community vote does not itself determine legal liability.

The license includes cure provisions for ordinary violations and stronger termination for deliberate prohibited use. Remediation should prioritize affected people, cessation of harm, evidence preservation, correction, data obligations, and prevention of recurrence.

## 13. Professional review notice

This is a custom license intended to express BootX's common-good inheritance. Custom ethical-use licenses can create uncertainty in enforceability, compatibility, contribution, distribution, patent, and jurisdictional questions.

Before material public distribution, institutional adoption, or High-Impact deployment, obtain review from a qualified intellectual-property lawyer familiar with software, human-rights conditions, and the relevant jurisdictions.

## 14. Final public baseline and private successor

Personal Companion `0.4.0-dev` and its accompanying repository documentation are the final planned publicly source-available development baseline. Future risk-management and decision-assistance development is intended to use a separately controlled private-source successor namespace, beginning with `0.5.0-private-dev` or later.

This is a prospective development and release-control policy, not an amendment to License 1.0. It does not revoke rights already granted for public material, convert public contributions into proprietary code, or authorize closed-source distribution. Private modification, external deployment, distribution, and relicensing can create different obligations.

Before representing or distributing a successor as closed source, the project must complete a rights-holder and third-party-license audit, determine whether separate relicensing authority exists, follow the applicable D4 governance process, and obtain qualified legal review. The controlling safeguards and designation checklist are in [RELEASE_BOUNDARY.md](RELEASE_BOUNDARY.md).
