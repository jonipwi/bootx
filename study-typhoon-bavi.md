# AI Contribution

Modern weather prediction is made possible by combining:

- Satellite observations
- Weather balloons
- Ocean buoys
- Ships
- Aircraft observations
- Doppler radar
- Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP)
- Artificial Intelligence

Rather than measuring every wind particle directly, AI continuously combines millions of observations with physical equations to simulate Earth's atmosphere.

The result is a continuously updated digital representation of global weather.

# Engineering Perspective

Large reservoirs and dams reduce flood risk but cannot eliminate it.

Safety depends upon:

- rainfall
- watershed conditions
- reservoir capacity
- controlled releases
- spillway design
- downstream evacuation

Engineering reduces risk.

It cannot eliminate natural hazards.

# Data Interpretation

One important lesson learned while monitoring Typhoon Bavi:

Wind maps show wind at a selected point.

That does NOT necessarily represent the storm's maximum sustained wind.

Correct interpretation requires context.

This principle also applies to:

- AI
- cybersecurity
- economics
- science
- public policy

Data without context may produce incorrect conclusions.
