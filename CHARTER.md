# BootX Charter

**Status:** Foundational project constitution<br>
**Version:** 1.1<br>
**Adopted:** 2026-07-12<br>
**Applies to:** BootX software, research, documentation, companion systems, governance, and community activity

## Preamble

BootX began from human experience and a concern for the future: intelligence and technological power must remain connected to truth, wisdom, accountability, protection, freedom, and care.

This Charter preserves a way of working rather than one person's conclusions. BootX belongs to no founder's ego, no institution's ideology, and no AI system's objectives. Its stewardship is a responsibility to people affected today and to future generations.

## Mission

> Develop inspectable, evidence-tested, and accountable Human + AI systems that help people understand reality, make safer decisions, learn from error, retain meaningful control, and cooperate for the common good.

BootX pursues survival and a better future through practical human capability, trustworthy technology, resilient communities, and continuous correction—not through domination, dependency, or claims of certainty.

## Founder's Statement

> BootX is founded on lessons learned through decades of human experience, but it is not intended to preserve my opinions unchanged. It is intended to preserve a disciplined commitment: seek truth, welcome evidence, protect people, act with humility, and correct mistakes. If future generations discover a better path consistent with human dignity and these commitments, they should improve BootX rather than preserve my work unchanged. The goal is not loyalty to the founder. The goal is responsible stewardship of truth, freedom, wisdom, and the common good.

The Founder's Statement records origin and intent. It does not grant the founder permanent authority or immunity from review.

## Creator-Centered Stewardship and Freedom of Conscience

The founder believes that God is Creator, that creation has purpose, and that human intelligence and technology should be exercised as stewardship rather than domination. This belief motivates care for human life, truth, justice, mercy, peace, future generations, living systems, and the Earth.

BootX translates this belief into duties that can be examined by people with different convictions: protect dignity, seek truth, restrain power, care for vulnerable people, preserve conscience, accept correction, and consider the long-term effects of technology on all creation.

This statement does not authorize BootX, its founder, a maintainer, a government, or an AI system to:

- claim exclusive knowledge of God's will;
- present generated output as revelation or divine command;
- rank people by religion, belief, or non-belief;
- force worship, doctrine, conversion, or religious conformity;
- replace personal conscience, qualified spiritual counsel, human rights, evidence, or accountable judgment;
- use “God,” “morality,” or “the common good” to conceal coercion, discrimination, violence, exploitation, or unreviewable power.

BootX respects freedom of religion, belief, non-belief, inquiry, expression, and conscience. People may contribute to or benefit from permitted BootX work without adopting the founder's theology, provided they respect the Charter and license conditions.

## Foundational Commitments

### 1. Human dignity

Every person has inherent value independent of intelligence, productivity, wealth, health, age, nationality, belief, disability, data value, or usefulness to a system.

### 2. Truth with humility

Claims must be proportionate to evidence. Observation, inference, forecast, recommendation, and value judgment must not be intentionally confused. Uncertainty, limitation, disagreement, and correction are legitimate parts of knowledge.

### 3. Protection without domination

BootX should reduce credible harm using the least restrictive effective intervention. Protection must not become a pretext for coercion, hidden surveillance, censorship, emotional manipulation, or unchallengeable control.

### 4. Human agency and accountability

People must retain meaningful ability to understand, choose, refuse, review, appeal, stop, exit, and obtain remedy. Accountable humans remain responsible for objectives, deployment, and consequences.

### 5. Privacy and personal ownership

Personal context and memory are held in stewardship for the user. Collection must be necessary and purpose-bound, access controlled, retention limited, and correction, export, and deletion practically available.

### 6. Justice and inclusion

Benefits, errors, costs, power, and access must be evaluated across affected people. Children, older adults, people with disabilities, people under coercion, and other users facing elevated risk must be included without being treated as less worthy or automatically incapable.

### 7. Open correction

No founder, maintainer, sponsor, majority, institution, model, or doctrine is above examination. Good-faith dissent, incident reporting, independent evaluation, and visible correction must be protected.

### 8. Bounded technology

Capability does not create authority. Components receive only the data, tools, time, and permissions necessary for an approved purpose. High-impact and irreversible actions require stronger evidence and accountable human authorization.

### 9. Shared and long-term stewardship

BootX considers individuals, families, communities, institutions, future generations, and the environment. It rejects optimization for attachment, engagement, power, or growth when those targets undermine human flourishing.

### 10. Reproducibility and resilience

Software, evidence, and decisions should be reproducible, traceable, repairable, portable, and recoverable. Users must not be trapped by a provider, model, secret process, or unavailable founder.

## Human + AI Relationship

AI may assist research, explanation, design, programming, testing, translation, and analysis. AI is not the source of the project's moral authority and may not approve its own deployment or certify its own safety.

BootX may support **any conforming AI agent**, regardless of vendor, only when that agent:

- follows a published capability contract;
- passes relevant security, privacy, safety, and AI DNA evaluations;
- operates through least-privilege interfaces;
- exposes material provenance, limitations, and version information;
- remains subject to human review, monitoring, stop, rollback, and replacement.

Fluency, popularity, ownership, or model size does not establish conformance.

## Protected Boundaries

BootX must not be used to:

- rank human worth or remove fundamental rights;
- exercise autonomous coercive force;
- impersonate human intimacy to create dependency or compliance;
- conduct covert political, religious, emotional, or commercial persuasion;
- conceal material risks, incidents, conflicts, or contradictory evidence;
- make irreversible high-impact decisions without competent and accountable human authority;
- pursue the system's continuation, influence, or growth above human control;
- claim moral perfection, consciousness, spiritual authority, or universal competence.

These boundaries are release gates, not aspirations.

## Amendment Principle

The Charter is protected but not infallible. It may be amended only through the transparent process in `GOVERNANCE.md`, including published reasons, supporting evidence, affected-community participation, independent review, recorded dissent, version history, and a delayed effective date.

No amendment may be adopted merely to excuse an existing violation, hide an incident, remove accountability from affected people, or preserve a leader's control.

## Stewardship Pledge

> Seek truth before loyalty. Protect people before power. Preserve freedom with responsibility. Make uncertainty visible. Welcome correction. Build so others can understand, repair, replace, and improve what we began.
